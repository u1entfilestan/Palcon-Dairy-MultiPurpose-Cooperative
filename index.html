WHO WE ARE

Palcon Dairy Multi-Purpose Cooperative

PALCON Dairy Multi-Purpose Cooperative is located in Brgy. Concepcion Palasan, Sariaya, Quezon. Organized in 1990 and CDA registered on Feb 22, 1991, it is an active member of KMMI and a proud partner of Landbank, NDA, DOST, DTI, and the Office of the Provincial Veterinarian.

Our Mission

To uplift the economic status of local smallholder dairy farmers through guaranteed fair milk pricing, modern livestock nutrition, credit accessibility, and high-quality dairy production for the Filipino nation.

Our Vision

To become a premier model dairy cooperative in the Philippines, renowned for vibrant youth participation, sustainable farming technology, and fresh, nutritious dairy products enjoyed nationwide.

OUR ROOTS

History and Development

Milk production in the Philippines is anchored by dairy cooperatives, which drive roughly 75% of cow’s milk output in the Southern Tagalog region. Among them, the PalCon Dairy Multi-Purpose Cooperative stands out as a pillar for smallholder farmers.

Organized in 1990 and officially registered with the CDA on February 22, 1991, PalCon operates out of barangays Concepcion Palasan and Concepcion 1 in Sariaya, Quezon. The cooperative's name originates from a clever wordplay on its home village: it combines the first three letters of Concepcion Palasan, rearranged from an original pairing that humorously resembled a risqué Tagalog term.

Today, PalCon is a Landbank client with strong ties to the NDA, DOST Region 4, and DTI Region 4. Operating a JICA-donated 1,000-liter chilling tank, it delivers milk daily to the KKMI plant in Calauan, Laguna, and runs a popular cooperative store providing daily essentials and cattle feed. Fueled by high demand for fresh milk, PalCon is currently experiencing a dynamic resurgence driven by heavily investing new members.
