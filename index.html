WHO WE ARE

Palcon Dairy Multi-Purpose Cooperative

PALCON Dairy Multi-Purpose Cooperative is located in Brgy. Concepcion Palasan, Sariaya, Quezon. Organized in 1990 and CDA registered on Feb 22, 1991, it is an active member of KMMI and a proud partner of Landbank, NDA, DOST, DTI, and the Office of the Provincial Veterinarian.

Our Mission

To uplift the economic status of local smallholder dairy farmers through guaranteed fair milk pricing, modern livestock nutrition, credit accessibility, and high-quality dairy production for the Filipino nation.

Our Vision

To become a premier model dairy cooperative in the Philippines, renowned for vibrant youth participation, sustainable farming technology, and fresh, nutritious dairy products enjoyed nationwide.

OUR ROOTS

History and Development

Organized in 1990 and officially registered with the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) on February 22, 1991, Palcon Dairy Multi-Purpose Cooperative has been serving smallholder farmers for over three decades. Operating out of Brgy. Concepcion Palasan, Sariaya, Quezon, the coop has been a Landbank client since 2015 and maintains strong ties with the National Dairy Authority (NDA), DOST Region 4, DTI Region 4, MCDC, and the Provincial Veterinary Office.
